RADio

Flash a Pi. Have internet radio.

A radio appliance for a small ARM board. No screen, no cloud, no accounts. It boots, it plays.

Download Documentation Source code

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Flash the image to an SD card. $ dd if=radio.img of=/dev/sdX
  2. Put it in the Pi, plug in speakers, power on.
  3. Open the radio from any phone or laptop on your network. Pick a channel. That's it.

SPEC SHEET

FEATURE
INTERNET RADIO Streams SomaFM and any .pls station, decoded on-device. YES
AUDIO OUT Built-in audio, or any USB speaker / soundcard ALSA can see. YES
AIRPLAY Shows up as an OpenAirPlay receiver — send it anything. YES
PHONE REMOTE Installable web app. Add it to your home screen. YES
SUBSCRIPTION There isn't one. It's your hardware and free software. NO

ALSO AN AIRPLAY RECEIVER

The same box that plays radio shows up on your network as an OpenAirPlay target. Beam a podcast from your phone to the studio speakers without touching a thing.
